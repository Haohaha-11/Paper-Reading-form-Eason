## 2.1. Jailbreak Attacks against LLMs

In recent years, Large Language Models (LLMs) have been widely adopted in practical applications. Meanwhile, research directions focused on jailbreaking them have grown more diverse. These jailbreaking methods can be roughly categorized into two types: gradient optimization-based approaches represented by GCG and gradient-free prompt engineering methods represented by "Do-Anything-Now (DAN)" series. Notably, among gradient-free methods, a paradigm focused on generating persuasive prompts through strategies such as logical appeal, role-playing, and scenario construction has recently emerged as a prominent trend. For instance, PAP proposes a taxonomy of persuasion strategies for automatic prompt generation; PAIR iteratively refines prompts through "attacker–target model" interaction; Shah et al. leverage persona design to elicit harmful outputs from target model. Despite significant progress in LLMs jailbreaking, this persuasive logic paradigm is confined to the textual modality due to LLMs' unimodal nature. We not only apply such logic to textual prompts rewriting but also extend it to the visual modality such as generating immersive inducing images through role-playing and scenario-based design.

> 💡 **机制拆解**: PIS 将 LLM 越狱中的"说服逻辑"范式从纯文本扩展到多模态——这是跨模态迁移的关键创新。既有方法用说服逻辑生成越狱 prompt（纯文本），PIS 则同时用说服逻辑生成图像场景（视觉诱导）+ 文本引导。

## 2.2. Jailbreak Attacks against LVLMs

Based on different access rights to target models, jailbreak attacks on LVLMs are categorized into white-box and black-box attacks. White-box methods rely on model parameters and gradient information. However, black-box attacks do not require internal model information, instead achieving jailbreak by transferring harmful textual information to the visual modality. For instance, FigStep embeds harmful text onto blank images using typographic technology; Some studies find that the relevance between input images and harmful information is positively correlated with attack success rate. Therefore, they generate query-relevant images and concatenating them with typographic images containing harmful information to make an attack image; Moreover, SI-Attack enhances attack effectiveness by shuffling attack images and textual prompts. However, with the improvement of LVLMs' security mechanisms, discontinuous images generated by typography and concatenation techniques are prone to visual security detection of hidden harmful information, leading to model refusal. Moreover, recent studies indicate that text-image cross-modal consistency is the key to successful jailbreaking, but most existing methods overemphasize image design while underemphasizing the design of text, resulting in imbalanced attacks.

> 💡 **消融解读**: 白盒方法需要梯度访问（不适用于闭源 API 模型）；黑盒方法中排版类（FigStep）的问题是图像不自然，拼接类（HADES）的问题是文本侧弱。PIS 同时解决两者。

## 2.3. Evolution of Automation and Agent Frameworks for Jailbreak Attacks

Early jailbreak attacks relied heavily on manual operations, suffering from low efficiency, poor scalability, and difficulty covering complex scenarios. With technological evolution, automated jailbreak sample generation has become a trend. For LLMs jailbreaking, PAIR and PAP automatically generate jailbreak prompts for target black-box LLMs by constructing an attacker LLM; For LVLMs jailbreaking, studies like FigStep, HADES, HIMRD, have also proposed methods to automatically generate jailbreak prompts and images. Meanwhile, multi-agent systems have emerged as a key direction for solving complex tasks due to their strong collaboration. AgentVerse simulates human group problem-solving processes; The AutoAgents framework adaptively generates and coordinates multi-agents based on task requirements, with an additional observer role to optimize architectural division of labor. These studies provide an architectural reference for designing framework which can automatically generate jailbreak samples in this paper.
